## Step 1 - Download the App

WindowShopper is free to download on Android and iOS.

- **Android:** [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.windowshopper.smartshopping)
- **iPhone / iPad:** [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/windowshopper/id6765528665)

---

## Step 2 - Create an Account

Open the app and sign up with your email address, or continue with your Google account for a one-tap sign-in. You'll be asked whether you're joining as a **shopper** or a **store owner**. You can always add a store later.

---

## Step 3 - Allow Location Access

WindowShopper uses your location to show stores and prices near you. When prompted, tap **Allow** to get relevant local results. If you'd rather not share your location, you can still search manually by entering a place name.

---

## For Shoppers

### Searching for a Product

Tap the search bar and type what you're looking for "cooking oil", "bread", "sugar", anything. The app will show you a list of nearby stores that carry it along with each store's current price, so you can see at a glance where the best deal is.

### Using the Map

Switch to the map tab to see all nearby stores as pins on a live map. Tap any pin to see the store's name, distance from you, and a snapshot of their products. This is useful for spotting clusters of stores in one area and planning your trip geographically.

### Building a Shopping List

Go to **Shopping Lists** and create a new list. Add each item you need you can type them one at a time or paste a whole list. Once you're done, tap **Find Cheapest** and the app automatically matches every item on your list to the lowest-priced option available across all nearby stores.

You can review each suggestion, swap out any item for a different store or price, and check things off as you go.

### Getting a Route

Once you've picked your stores, tap **Get Route**. The app calculates the most efficient order to visit them and gives you turn-by-turn directions so you're never backtracking unnecessarily.

### Scanning a Receipt

Open the **Receipt Scanner**, point your camera at any paper receipt, and tap capture. The app reads every item and price on the receipt automatically. This helps you keep track of what you're spending and which stores offer the best value over time.

### Sharing a Deal

On any product or store page, tap the **Share** button to send a link via WhatsApp, SMS, or any other app. The person receiving it can tap the link and land straight on that page in WindowShopper.

## For Store Owners

### Setting Up Your Store

After creating an account, select **I'm a store owner** and follow the setup steps: add your store name, category, contact details, and a brief description. If you have a physical location, drop a pin on the map. If you sell online only, skip the location step. your store will still appear in search results.

Once submitted, your store goes through a quick approval process before it appears publicly.

### Adding Products

Go to your store dashboard and tap **Add Product**. Fill in the product name, category, price, and upload a photo. Your listing goes live immediately for nearby shoppers to find.

Have lots of products? Use the **Bulk Upload** option to import them all at once from a spreadsheet.

### Keeping Prices Current

Shoppers rely on your prices being accurate. Whenever you change a price, update it in the app so customers always see the right number. Updated prices are reflected in search results in real time.

### Recording Sales

Use the **Sales** tab in your dashboard to log what you've sold. Over time this builds up a picture of your best-selling products and busiest periods, all visible in your analytics dashboard.

### Running a Promotion

To offer a temporary discount, go to the product you want to promote, tap **Add Promotion**, set the discounted price and end date. Promoted products get highlighted in search results for the duration.

---

## Tips

- **Set your search radius** in Settings to control how far out the app looks for stores. A smaller radius is faster for quick local trips; a wider one is useful when you're willing to travel for a better deal.
- **Switch currencies** in Settings if you travel between Zimbabwe, Kenya, and South Africa.
- **QR codes** - every store and product has one. Tap Share → QR Code to generate it. Print it on a flyer, post it on social media, or stick it on your window.
