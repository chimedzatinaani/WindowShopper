## The Problem

In most cities across Africa, retail is made up of hundreds of small, independent stores, supermarkets, shops, market stalls and wholesalers, each with their own prices, stock and promotions. None of that information is online. If you want to know whether the supermarket down the road is cheaper than the one across town, you have to physically go and check.

That costs time, transport money and energy, and most people just accept it.

---

## What We're Building

WindowShopper is a price comparison and shopping app built specifically for this reality. It brings local store inventories online so that shoppers can search, compare, and plan their shopping trips from their phone before they leave home.

It's not a delivery app. It's not an e-commerce platform. It's a tool that makes physical shopping smarter, faster, and cheaper by giving you the information you need upfront.

---

## Who It's For

**Shoppers** who shop at local stores and want to stretch their money further without spending hours hunting for the best price.

**Store owners** from large supermarkets to small independent shops who want to reach nearby customers who are actively looking for what they sell.

## The Vision

Every shopper, everywhere, should be able to know the price of something before they go to buy it. What feels like a basic expectation in some parts of the world is still a daily friction point for millions of people in emerging markets.

WindowShopper exists to close that gap of one store, one product, one saved trip at a time.
