## For Shoppers

### Find Products Near You
Search for any product — groceries, household goods, electronics and see a list of nearby stores that carry it, each showing the current price and how far away the store is. No more calling around or driving to find out something is out of stock.

### Compare Prices on the Map
Switch to the map view and see every store near you as a pin. Tap any store to preview what they stock and at what price. You can visually spot which part of town has the best deals before you go anywhere.

### Build a Smart Shopping List
Add everything you need to buy by typing items one by one or pasting a whole list at once. The app parses quantities automatically (e.g. "milk x2" picks up that you need two). Once your list is ready, tap **Find Cheapest** and the app scans all nearby stores and selects the best-priced option for every single item on your list.

### Plan Your Route
After choosing your stores, the app calculates the most efficient order to visit them so you're not doubling back across town. Supports both walking and driving directions.

### Scan Receipts with Your Camera
Point your phone at any paper receipt and the app reads it automatically every item, every price, in seconds. Useful for tracking what you've spent and for discovering which stores consistently offer better value over time.

### See Price History
On any product page, scroll down to see how the price has changed over time across different stores. Helpful for spotting seasonal price spikes and knowing when you're actually getting a good deal.

### Share Stores and Products
Found a great deal? Share it with a link or a QR code straight from the app. Anyone who taps the link goes directly to that store or product page, even if they haven't used WindowShopper before.

## For Store Owners

### Put Your Store on the Map
Create a store profile with your name, location, contact details, and the categories you sell. Once approved, your store appears on the map for nearby shoppers who are actively looking to buy.

### No Physical Store? No Problem
Online-only sellers can list products without a physical address. Your store shows up in search results and on product pages — just without a map pin.

### List Your Products
Add products one at a time with a name, price, category, and photos. Upload multiple images per product so shoppers can see exactly what they're getting. Keep prices up to date and your listing stays relevant.

### Upload in Bulk
Have a lot of products to add? Upload a spreadsheet (CSV or Excel) and the app creates all your listings at once. Ideal for stores with large or frequently changing inventories.

### Run Promotions
Set a discounted price on any product for a limited time. Promoted products get extra visibility in search results, helping you move stock and attract new customers.

### Track Your Sales
Log sales directly in the app and view your daily, weekly, and monthly revenue. See which products are selling best and make smarter stocking decisions.

### Share Your Store
Every store gets a unique link and QR code you can print, share on social media, or put on a business card. Customers tap it and land straight on your store profile in the app.

---

## For Everyone

### Multiple Currencies
The app supports local currencies across Zimbabwe, Kenya, South Africa, etc. Set your preferred currency in settings and all prices display accordingly.

### Search Radius Control
Decide how wide your search net is, that is, a few blocks or across the whole town. Adjust the search radius in settings to match how far you're willing to travel.

### Deep Links & QR Codes
Every store and product page has a unique link and QR code. Share them anywhere, on WhatsApp, SMS, social media and the link opens directly in the app.
