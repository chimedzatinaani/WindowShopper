## What is WindowShopper?

WindowShopper is a free mobile app that helps everyday shoppers find the cheapest prices at stores near them without having to visit each one in person.

Browse local store inventories, compare prices on the products you need, build a shopping list and let the app tell you exactly where to go and in what order. Stop guessing. Stop overpaying.

Available now on **Android** and **iOS**.

## Who is it for?

### Shoppers
You're tired of visiting three stores only to find out the first one had the best price. WindowShopper shows you prices upfront so you can plan a smarter trip, save money, and get home faster.

### Store Owners
You want more foot traffic and a way to show nearby customers what you stock and at what price. WindowShopper puts your store on the map and lets shoppers find you when they're actively looking to buy.

---

## What can you do with it?

- **Search for any product** and instantly see which nearby stores carry it and at what price
- **Compare prices across stores** on a live map
- **Build a shopping list** and let the app find the cheapest option for every item automatically
- **Get a route** that takes you to all your chosen stores in the most efficient order
- **Scan a receipt** with your camera and the app reads every item and price for you
- **Share a store or product** with a link or QR code so others can find it too
